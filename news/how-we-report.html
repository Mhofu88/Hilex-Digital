HILEX NEWS STANDARD

Explain first. Verify always.

Our five questions

1. What happened? State the development and its status accurately.

2. Why does it matter? Translate complexity without exaggeration.

3. Who may be affected? Identify relevant audiences.

4. What is the African context? Never imply foreign rules automatically become African law.

5. Where can I verify it? Prefer primary and official sources and date fast-changing information.

Corrections: Material changes should update the story and its last-verified date.
