AFRICAN DIGITAL ASSET REGULATION • STAGE 3

🏢 Business compliance in Africa

Practical due diligence for digital merchants and platform developers.

Educational status • 21 October 2026: Regulatory requirements are jurisdiction- and activity-specific. Verify current primary documents before relying on any statement.

Identify the activity

Distinguish education and software development from custody, brokerage, exchange, payments, investment advice and remittances. A technical platform can trigger financial regulation depending on what it actually does.

Build safeguards

Map KYC/KYB and AML/CFT duties where applicable; protect customer information; maintain transaction records; publish accurate pricing, risk warnings and complaint channels.

Cross-border operations

Review licensing, foreign-exchange, tax, sanctions, privacy and consumer obligations for each country served. Hosting a website abroad does not necessarily remove local requirements.

Practical scenario

A merchant directory or e-commerce platform may need privacy and consumer safeguards even if it never handles crypto. Adding custody or payments features can create further obligations.

Technology provider boundary

A software development provider can build tools and documentation, but cannot promise that a client’s business will be legally compliant.
