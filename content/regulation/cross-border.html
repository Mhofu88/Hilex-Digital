Cross-Border Compliance

Independent educational guidance. Verify current requirements through the relevant official authorities.

What this means

Selling digitally can connect a small African business with customers beyond its home market, but crossing borders can introduce additional responsibilities.

Practical rule: Consider payment and foreign-exchange rules, taxes, consumer rights, data transfers, prohibited transactions and any applicable trade restrictions. Expansion should follow verification, not guesswork.
