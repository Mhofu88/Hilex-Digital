Digital-Asset Regulation

Independent educational guidance. Verify current requirements through the relevant official authorities.

What this means

Digital assets can be treated differently depending on their characteristics and how they are used. Rules may apply to exchanges, custody, payments, investment services, promotions or other activities.

Practical rule: Do not assume that one country’s rules apply across Africa. Check current official sources before relying on claims that a particular asset, platform or activity is approved, licensed, prohibited or unregulated.
