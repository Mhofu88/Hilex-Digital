Regulation Basics

Independent educational guidance. Verify current requirements through the relevant official authorities.

What this means

Digital activity can be governed by more than one type of rule. The applicable requirements depend on the country, the service being offered, who provides it and how money or personal information moves.

Practical rule: Before launching or using a digital service, identify the activity, the jurisdiction and the relevant official authority. A technology being available online does not automatically mean every use of it is permitted or unregulated.
