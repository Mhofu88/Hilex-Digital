Tax & Business Records

Independent educational guidance. Verify current requirements through the relevant official authorities.

What this means

Digital transactions are still business transactions. Good records help a business understand income, expenses, customers and obligations.

Practical rule: Keep reliable transaction records and supporting documents. Tax treatment can differ by country and by type of transaction, including digital-asset transactions, so verify current guidance with the relevant revenue authority or qualified adviser.
