🇿🇼 Zimbabwe: regulation and regulatory status

Zimbabwean institutions, payments and cautious interpretation of public guidance.

Educational status • 21 October 2026: Regulatory requirements are jurisdiction- and activity-specific. Verify current primary documents before relying on any statement.

Status and scope

Zimbabwe’s legal treatment of crypto-assets must be assessed by activity: holding, exchanging, advertising, payments, remittances and offering services may raise different questions. A regulatory consultation, sandbox announcement or policy proposal is not the same as a law or an authorisation.

Institutions to check

The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ) is an essential source for payment systems, currency and exchange-control information. Other applicable obligations may involve financial intelligence, tax, consumer protection and data protection authorities.

Practical scenario

A Harare merchant considering digital-asset payments should first confirm the current legal treatment of the proposed payment method, foreign-exchange implications, customer disclosures and record-keeping duties. Do not infer approval from a wallet app or an exchange listing.

Evidence boundary

This lesson does not assert that cryptocurrency is legal tender, that a particular exchange is licensed, or that a general-purpose virtual-asset licence exists. Check the latest dated primary documents before taking action.
