Consumer Protection

Independent educational guidance. Verify current requirements through the relevant official authorities.

What this means

Digital businesses should communicate prices, important conditions, risks and contact information clearly. Customers should know who they are dealing with and what they are agreeing to.

Practical rule: Keep evidence of transactions and customer communications. Avoid misleading claims, hidden charges and guarantees that cannot be supported. Check the consumer-protection rules that apply in the customer’s market.
