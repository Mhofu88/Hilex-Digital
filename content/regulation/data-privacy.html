Data & Privacy

Independent educational guidance. Verify current requirements through the relevant official authorities.

What this means

Names, phone numbers, identity documents, addresses and payment information can create responsibilities for the organisation collecting them.

Practical rule: Collect only what you genuinely need, restrict access, secure stored information and understand when consent or another lawful basis is required. Data-protection requirements differ across African jurisdictions.
