06 • NEW-TO-PI PATHWAY

From HILEX Learner to Pi Participant

Independent HILEX education. Verify current Pi-specific instructions through official Pi Network sources.

This pathway is designed for someone who discovers Pi through HILEX but is not yet a Pioneer.

Step 1 — Learn before registering

Understand what Pi Network is, how wallet responsibility works, what KYC means and how ecosystem apps differ from official Pi products.

Step 2 — Use CHIGALEX1 External for deeper community education

CHIGALEX1 External can provide Pi-focused learning on the normal web without requiring the learner to begin inside a Pi Browser-only environment.

Step 3 — Verify official onboarding instructions

If the learner decides to participate, registration, Pi Browser, KYC and wallet steps should be completed using current official Pi Network guidance and official applications.

Step 4 — Enter the ecosystem deliberately

Once the user is eligible and comfortable with Pi Browser, they can explore ecosystem applications and deeper Pi-focused education.

Step 5 — CHIGALEX1 inside the Pi ecosystem

For users who choose Pi participation, the internal CHIGALEX1 app can become a deeper education and business/merchant environment designed around Pi ecosystem use.

Important: HILEX should never make registration, investment or participation sound compulsory. Its job is to help people understand enough to make their own informed decision.
