KYC, Mainnet & Access

Independent HILEX education. Verify current Pi-specific instructions through official Pi Network sources.

Several terms are often mixed together. They should be understood separately.

KYC

KYC is identity verification for individual users. Eligibility, timing and process details should be checked inside official Pi systems.

Mainnet wallet access

Pi Network has introduced pathways that can allow eligible verified participants to activate a Mainnet wallet. Wallet access and Mainnet migration are not necessarily the same process.

Mainnet migration

Migration refers to moving an eligible Pioneer balance to Mainnet after applicable requirements are met.

KYB

KYB is business verification and is different from individual KYC. Pi Network states that the appropriate route can depend on the type and scale of the business; some small operators may participate using a KYC-verified individual's wallet where appropriate, while businesses that require a Pi Mainnet business wallet need to follow applicable KYB requirements.
