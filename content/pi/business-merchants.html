Businesses & Merchants

Independent HILEX education. Verify current Pi-specific instructions through official Pi Network sources.

Businesses should approach Pi as they would any emerging payment or Web3 channel: understand customer need, operational requirements, record keeping, security, taxation and local regulatory obligations before committing resources.

Start with the business case

  • Are customers asking to transact through Pi?
  • Can staff reconcile transactions and maintain records?
  • Is the wallet arrangement appropriate for the legal form and scale of the business?
  • Does local law or tax treatment create additional obligations?

KYC and KYB are not interchangeable

Pi's official guidance distinguishes individual KYC from business KYB.

CHIGALEX1 as the deeper Pi-business pathway

HILEX can introduce businesses to Pi education. Businesses that choose to explore Pi further can continue through CHIGALEX1's Pi-focused education and merchant resources, while official verification and wallet processes remain under Pi Network's systems.
