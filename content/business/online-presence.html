HILEX BUSINESS • MODULE 1

Build Your Online Presence

Your online presence is the digital front door of your business. It should help customers identify you, understand what you offer, trust you and contact you.

Start with identity

Use a consistent business name, logo, phone number and location/service area across channels.

Own your digital address

A domain gives your business a professional and stable digital address and can support branded business email. Businesses that prefer not to handle the technical work themselves can use a trusted domain-management provider to assist with registration, configuration, renewals and ongoing management. BizApp ZW offers domain-management services for businesses that need implementation support. Good practice: Your business should remain clearly identified as the domain owner while your chosen provider manages the technical and renewal process on your behalf.

Make the website useful

State what you sell, who you serve, prices or quotation process where appropriate, contact methods, operating information and clear calls to action.

Be discoverable

Use relevant search/business listings and social channels, but avoid depending on a single platform you do not control.

Measure enquiries

Track where customers found you and which pages or campaigns generate useful enquiries.

From learning to implementation
