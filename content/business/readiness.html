HILEX BUSINESS

Digital Readiness Check

Answer all ten questions. This is a practical self-check, not a certification.

1. Does your business have a professional website or online business page?
2. Do you use a domain-based business email or another clearly managed professional contact channel?
3. Can customers pay you through at least one suitable digital payment method?
4. Do you keep organised digital sales, expense or customer records?
5. Do you regularly back up important business data?
6. Do important business accounts use strong unique passwords and two-factor authentication where available?
7. Can you recognise common phishing, impersonation and payment-redirection scams?
8. Can customers reliably contact your business online and receive timely responses?
9. Can customers view your products or services digitally through a catalogue, website or storefront?
10. If you want customers outside your home market, have you considered payments, delivery, currency, tax and regulatory requirements?
Next step: Use your result to choose which HILEX Business modules to study. A low score is simply a starting point.
