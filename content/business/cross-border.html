Cross-Border Digital Trade

Digital channels can expose an African SME to regional and international customers, but a website does not remove borders, costs or rules.

Validate demand first

Test whether customers outside your home market actually want the offer before investing heavily in expansion.

Plan fulfilment

Understand delivery times, customs processes, returns, insurance and who bears each cost.

Plan payment and currency

Consider settlement currency, conversion costs, payment availability, refunds and fraud controls.

Check compliance

Tax, consumer protection, data, product, licensing and financial rules vary by country and activity.

Think regionally, execute carefully

Regional integration can create opportunity, but expansion works best when operations, support and compliance can scale with demand.
