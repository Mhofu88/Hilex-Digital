E-commerce & Digital Marketing

Digital selling is more than posting products. Customers need clear information, confidence, a way to order, a way to pay and reliable fulfilment.

Create a useful catalogue

Use clear photos, descriptions, prices or quotation instructions, availability and contact/order buttons.

Build trust

Show accurate business details, delivery/collection information, return policies where relevant and realistic promises.

Turn attention into action

Every campaign should lead somewhere useful: product page, enquiry form, catalogue, booking or checkout.

Respond consistently

Fast, organised responses can matter as much as advertising. Use templates and customer records without becoming robotic.

Learn from data

Track enquiries, conversion, repeat customers and campaign costs rather than judging marketing only by likes.
