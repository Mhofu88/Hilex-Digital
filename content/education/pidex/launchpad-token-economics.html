Pi Launchpad and token economics

A launch mechanism can help distribute ecosystem tokens and establish initial liquidity; it cannot manufacture lasting demand.

English edition · Independent education · No investment recommendation

Module 5 of 6

Utility first

A token should solve a real problem. A hypothetical DigitalMall DMT could represent a marketplace benefit, but its rights, supply, issuer and limitations would need clear disclosure. DMT is an educational example, not a live offer.

Liquidity and supply

Initial pools can enable trading. Concentrated ownership, unlocks, low demand and shallow pools can produce volatile prices. A token priced in PI also inherits exposure to PI price changes.

Responsible development

Test with Test-Pi only where supported, document risks, check local laws and official eligibility, and validate customer demand before considering issuance. Do not promise returns or redemption.

Knowledge check

Does a token launch guarantee useful adoption?

No. Adoption depends on genuine demand and a working product.
