Understanding KYB exchanges

KYB means Know Your Business: verification of a business, not a guarantee of its solvency or the safety of every trade.

English edition · Independent education · No investment recommendation

Module 1 of 6

Why exchanges matter

Centralized exchanges match buyers and sellers, provide observable prices and can connect native Pi to other currencies. Onramps offer another route into the ecosystem. Pi Network’s Open Network announcement explicitly recognizes these routes.

What KYB does not mean

Verification is not a deposit guarantee, investment endorsement or permission to operate in every country. Check the official KYB list, local rules, withdrawal availability, fees and custody risks.

Rural merchant example

A shopkeeper comparing a mobile-money payment with a Pi payment needs to know the actual amount received after fees and whether suppliers accept it. A quoted exchange price alone does not guarantee conversion.

Knowledge check

Does KYB guarantee that an exchange cannot fail?

No. Business verification does not remove custody, market or operational risk.
