Four kinds of value

An agreed price is voluntary; a quoted price is displayed; a market price reflects executed trades; a redeemable value is what can actually be converted. The community GCV figure of $314,159 is not established by a configuration file.

What stability requires

Stable-value systems need credible mechanisms, liquidity and confidence. Some use reserves and redemption; others use different mechanisms and can fail. AI or cryptographic hashing alone cannot establish purchasing power.

Everyday purchasing power

A farmer accepting digital payment needs to compare what it buys in seed, transport and food, after fees. A private barter agreement does not guarantee the same value elsewhere. HILEX does not endorse a fixed PI valuation.

Knowledge check

Does a community agreement guarantee dollar redemption?

No. Redemption requires an actual counterparty and a functioning conversion mechanism.
