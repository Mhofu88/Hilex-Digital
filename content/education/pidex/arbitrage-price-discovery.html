KYB exchanges, arbitrage and price discovery

Connected markets can influence each other; neither automatically dictates the other’s price.

English edition · Independent education · No investment recommendation

Module 4 of 6

External and internal prices

An exchange may quote PI in dollars or stablecoins. A Pi-denominated DEX pool may quote an ecosystem token in PI. If a hypothetical token costs 5 PI and PI trades at $0.20, its implied reference is $1, not a guaranteed redemption price.

Arbitrage explained

If executable PI prices are $0.10 and $0.15 in two connected markets, buying pressure in the cheaper market and selling pressure in the dearer market may narrow the gap. This requires transferable native PI, a realizable conversion route, liquidity and costs below the difference. Pi-only pairs do not by themselves establish a dollar market.

Limits and feedback

Withdrawal delays, restrictions, fees, thin pools and price manipulation can prevent convergence. Real merchant demand may influence both markets. A declared price is not price discovery.

Knowledge check

Can arbitrage guarantee identical prices?

No. Frictions and limited liquidity can sustain differences.
