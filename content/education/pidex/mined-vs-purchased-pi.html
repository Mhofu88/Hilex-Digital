Mined Pi versus purchased Pi

Acquisition history and asset identity are different concepts.

English edition · Independent education · No investment recommendation

Module 2 of 6

One native asset

Migrated mining rewards and genuine native PI withdrawn from an exchange to Pi Mainnet are units of the same native asset. An exchange balance may instead be a custodial claim until withdrawal. IOUs and unrelated tokens are not native PI.

History and eligibility

Blockchain transactions are traceable. An application may impose eligibility or compliance rules, but that is not evidence of a network-wide second currency or blanket rejection of purchased Pi.

Check the evidence

A screenshot of a YAML file assigning a price or rejecting exchange coins is not proof of an official deployed protocol. Verify repository ownership, release documentation and actual network rules. Never enter a wallet passphrase into a verification site.

Knowledge check

Does buying native PI automatically make it a different currency?

No. The acquisition route does not change the native asset identity.
