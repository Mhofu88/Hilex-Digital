Understanding PiDEX and AMMs

A decentralized exchange allows on-chain swaps under protocol rules; an automated market maker (AMM) can use pooled assets rather than matching individual orders.

English edition · Independent education · No investment recommendation

Module 3 of 6

Liquidity pools

A simple illustrative constant-product pool holds 100 PI and 200 tokens. Its initial marginal quote is 2 tokens per PI. Adding 10 PI without fees leaves about 181.82 tokens in the pool, so the trader receives about 18.18 tokens, not 20.

Slippage and risk

Larger orders relative to pool size change the execution price. Fees, thin liquidity, malicious tokens and liquidity-provider losses matter. A pool does not guarantee stable prices.

Pi-specific status

Pi has published experimental DEX and AMM designs. Treat exact Mainnet features, supported pairs and launch timing as unconfirmed here; consult official updates before using any feature.

Knowledge check

Why might a swap return less than the displayed quote?

The trade changes pool balances; fees and slippage may also reduce proceeds.
