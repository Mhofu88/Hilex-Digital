Digital first steps for everyone

Learn with a basic phone, limited data or no bank account. No cryptocurrency purchase is needed.

English edition · Independent education · No investment recommendation

1. Start with what you have

A basic phone can support calls, SMS and some mobile-money services. A smartphone with a browser can open these lessons. Ask a trusted community facilitator to print or read the lessons aloud if internet access is limited.

2. Protect your money

Never share a PIN, one-time code or wallet recovery phrase. Verify the recipient and total fees before confirming a payment. If someone promises guaranteed profits, stop and check independently.

3. Understand mobile money

Mobile money may allow payments and cash-in or cash-out through authorized agents, depending on local rules and identification requirements. Ask about fees, receipts and complaint channels. You do not need crypto to learn digital finance.

Read about mobile money

4. Learn together

Community groups can discuss one lesson at a time. Practice with imaginary amounts rather than real money. Use the browser print option to share a paper copy. On supported browsers, previously loaded lessons may be available offline.
